Working well to preserve
6

Render failures now have scoped recovery.

Error recoveryWorking well

Evidence. App.tsx wraps the root and player in separate MobileErrorBoundary scopes. components/MobileErrorBoundary.tsx renders a labelled retry fallback and records the failure without clearing persisted profiles, catalog, progress, credentials, or connection data.

Why it works. A player render failure can return to detail without taking the library down, while a root failure offers controlled retry instead of a blank app.

What to preserve. Keep recovery scopes narrow and never turn a generic retry into broad data deletion.

7

Diagnostics are bounded and sanitized.

TrustWorking well

Evidence. mobileDiagnostics.ts provides the shared non-fatal path; Settings can list, export, and clear events. mobileDiagnosticPolicy.ts caps storage at 100 events, seven days, and 256 KB, while tests prove credential, secret-query, and local-path redaction.

Why it works. Operational failures now leave a useful local record without turning diagnostics into a second secret store.

What to preserve. Keep expected parser fallbacks out of the diagnostic stream and maintain the byte cap as event context grows.

8

LAN connection is automatic, bounded, and identity-safe.

User controlWorking well

Evidence. mobileLanClient.ts defines 4-second health/config, 12-second standard, 20-second library, and 45-second stream-preparation budgets. useMobileBonjourDiscovery.ts continuously rescans without clearing stable results and tolerates brief mDNS removals. mobileDiscoveryExperience.ts selects the single obvious desktop or the saved host identity automatically. mobileSecureTransportUrl.ts preserves the native loopback session secret. The desktop advertiser restarts with bounded backoff, and networkInfo.ts prefers physical private interfaces over VPN and virtual adapters.

Why it works. A first run connects without a mobile tap or desktop prompt. Owners can optionally require approval for new devices in Network settings. Later runs follow the saved desktop across DHCP changes, hide transient network failures behind quiet reconnecting state, and keep certificate changes approval-gated.

What to preserve. Keep automatic selection limited to one unambiguous host, keep approval optional and off by default, preserve certificate pinning, and keep manual address/PIN entry as a compact fallback rather than the primary journey.

9

Decode and persistence boundaries have contracts.

Error preventionWorking well

Evidence. Dedicated tests now cover valid, malformed, wrong-typed, truncated, and oversized payloads. mobileOfflineCache.ts persists catalog revisions separately from progress, updates last_seen_at for active paths, and expires only orphaned progress. The final mobile source suite passes 68 of 68 tests.

Why it works. Unchanged catalogs avoid full rewrites, active titles retain resume positions, malformed desktop data cannot silently enter state, and stale progress has explicit semantics.

What to preserve. Keep catalog revision ownership immutable and add fixtures whenever the desktop payload schema changes.

10

Others is excluded at the core selector.

ConsistencyWorking well

Evidence. mobileLibrary.ts exposes coreItems, which returns anime, TV, and movies only. App.tsx uses that selector for the core collection, while Others keeps its own direct-play route and remains available for thumbnail caching and ID ownership where needed.

Why it works. Home, hero, anime, TV, movies, search, My List, and continue-watching no longer each need to remember a local filter.

What to preserve. Keep coreItems as the only core-surface source and make any collection that includes Others explicit in its name and purpose.

11

Release generation and product scope are explicit.

Help and documentationWorking well

Evidence. Clean prebuild, CocoaPods integration, unsigned Android Release, and unsigned arm64 iOS Simulator Release all pass. The CI workflow now installs pods and targets LoomTVMobile.xcworkspace/LoomTVMobile. MOBILE_PRODUCT_SCOPE.md records background playback, lock-screen controls, picture-in-picture, English-only copy, and deep links as unsupported for this release; app.json declares no custom scheme.

Why it works. Generated native projects remain reproducible, path-space build defects are fixed in the config plugin, and absent product capabilities are decisions rather than accidental claims.

What to preserve. Revisit a scope row only with implementation, user-facing behavior, automated contracts, and device evidence.

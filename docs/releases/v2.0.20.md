# LoomTV 2.0.20 release notes

Reworks Discover and add-on catalogs around one shared layout, including the Archive.org page, and moves Settings onto shared layout and dialog components. Removes the Settings v2 and v3 previews. Stremio add-ons can now supply subtitles to the player and offer YouTube or external sources that open in the browser.

Playback information in the player now lists the video's resolution, format, bit depth, frame rate, dynamic range, bitrate and container. The player's episode panel shows full episode titles and reworks its playing-episode highlight, season pills and rating badges. Subtitle appearance and playback settings are reorganized.

Performance: movie credits analysis decodes HEVC, AV1 and VP9 with VideoToolbox on macOS, using about one eighth of the CPU time for identical results. Large Live TV guides are read in chunks, so a 200,000-entry guide no longer pauses the app for half a second or more than doubles its memory. Library artwork is released after the window has been hidden for 20 seconds.

This release publishes Electron desktop installers for macOS, Windows, and Linux. It does not publish Tauri or mobile binaries.

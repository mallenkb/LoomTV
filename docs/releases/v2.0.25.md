# LoomTV 2.0.25 release notes

The seek bar now marks intros, recaps, outros, credits, and previews in a light tint of your accent color, so you can see where they are before the Skip button appears. The knob on the seek bar uses your accent color.

Subtitles have their own tray, opened from the subtitles button. It has two tabs: Subtitles for your tracks, and Open Subtitles, which searches as soon as you open it and keeps its results when you switch tabs. Both tabs include the Appearance controls, and online subtitle timing is now under More options. The settings button opens a separate tray with Audio and Video.

Every slider uses one design: a label and value on top, a thicker track, and a small pill knob. Changed values turn your accent color and show a reset button. Notches mark zero, the default, and the subtitle positions, and dragging near one snaps to it. Tick guides appear only while you drag or use the arrow keys. Volume uses the same slider. Audio delay and subtitle timing now read "0.50s later", "0.25s earlier", or "In sync".

Subtitles no longer jump far up when the playback controls appear. They rise only as much as needed to clear the controls and settle back when the controls hide. Raised and High sit lower than before, and a Height slider under More options fine-tunes the position.

You can rescan intro and credits for the episode you're watching from Playback settings > Advanced, or for a season or the whole show from the new ⋯ menu on each season row. New markers load onto the seek bar when the rescan finishes.

Switching "Rename files after sync" to Automatically now organizes your files right away instead of waiting for the next sync.

Settings > About > Legal now lists TheIntroDB, AniSkip, and SkipDB, the services LoomTV uses for skip markers.

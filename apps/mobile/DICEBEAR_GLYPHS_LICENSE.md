# DiceBear Glyphs avatar attribution

LoomTV includes and displays avatars generated with the DiceBear Glyphs style.

- Work: “Abstract Avatars for All Creative Profile Use”
- Creator: Matt Houser
- Source: https://www.figma.com/community/file/1249154526125777853
- DiceBear style: https://www.dicebear.com/styles/glyphs/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

LoomTV applies selectable flat profile colors to the generated avatars and may resize them for display.
